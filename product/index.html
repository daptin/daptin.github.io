One connected backend

Your product has one reality. Its backend should too.

Customers, teams, records, files, actions, messages, and outside services all describe the same product. Daptin keeps them in one shared application context, so every new capability begins with more understanding and less integration work.

Explore the productSee complete product stories
The problem

A “modern stack” can become a collection of teams translating between services.

The database knows the records. The identity service knows the people. Object storage knows the files. A job runner knows the schedules. A provider integration knows an outside account. None of them automatically knows which customer owns the work or which rule should apply.

Every product feature then needs glue: copy identity into another service, recreate permissions, move secrets, reconcile failures, and explain the same business idea in several contracts.

Daptin takes a different position. Start from the application's records and relationships, then let identity, access, files, actions, live updates, storage, mail, and protocols build around that shared foundation.

What changes

Each capability arrives with context.

The value is not that many things fit in one binary. The value is that they can reuse the same understanding of people, data, ownership, and product behavior.

PeopleThe account is part of the model.

A customer or teammate can own records, join groups, connect outside accounts, and carry one identity into actions.

ContentThe file belongs to a real subject.

A document or image stays attached to its customer, project, article, permission, and workflow.

BehaviorThe action speaks product language.

Approve, invite, publish, send, or synchronize becomes a backend operation that several clients can reuse.

OperationsThe system remains yours to understand.

Database, storage, limits, health, certificates, and recovery stay visible operating choices.

What you own less of

Spend custom engineering on what makes the product different.

Daptin takes responsibility for repeatable backend foundations. Your team still owns product decisions and the infrastructure boundary.

Daptin provides the connected foundation.

  • Relational records, relationships, and public interfaces
  • Accounts, groups, ownership, and permission mechanisms
  • Files, cloud-store connections, and hosted content
  • Named actions, schedules, state, templates, and exchange
  • Realtime, collaboration, integrations, mail, feeds, and protocols
  • Database adapters, runtime controls, health, and distributed coordination

Your team defines and operates the product.

  • The vocabulary, rules, and experience customers should see
  • Which capabilities fit and how they compose
  • Safe access defaults and administrator practices
  • Database, storage, secrets, DNS, TLS, capacity, and backups
  • Provider compatibility, costs, terms, and failure handling
  • Monitoring, incident response, upgrades, and recovery
Fit

Choose Daptin for connected product foundations—not every possible backend.

A clear boundary is more useful than a universal claim.

Daptin is a strong fit when…

Your product has relational data plus several recurring needs: customer identity, team access, files, backend actions, schedules, live views, provider connections, mail, sites, or established protocols. You value self-hosting and want those parts to share context.

Choose something else when…

Nearly every request is bespoke compute, you need a best-in-class specialist system for one function, a managed provider must own operations, or your workload cannot fit the released compatibility and scaling boundaries.
