Why it matters

Reusable content needs more structure than a filename and a folder.

A policy document, collaborative note, media set, or customer package may be referenced by several people and workflows. Treating it only as a provider object makes those relationships hard to express.

Daptin includes document and collection records as part of its standard application foundation. They can participate in ordinary relationships, permissions, file storage, APIs, and higher-level experiences.

That gives product teams a place to model what the content is and why it matters, while storage remains responsible for the bytes.

Know the boundary

What Daptin leaves in your hands.

Documents and collections are foundational records, not a complete document-management or records-retention suite. Versioning policy, legal holds, full-text search, editorial workflow, and compliance remain separate design choices.
