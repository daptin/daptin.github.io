Why it matters

A team account is more than a login and a role string.

In a collaborative product, people belong to organizations, projects, or workspaces. They create records, share files, invite colleagues, and leave. If membership lives in one service and product data in another, every feature has to reconcile the two.

Daptin keeps accounts, groups, ownership, and relationships in the same application model. A group can represent a team or workspace, while ordinary relationships connect its members to the records they work on.

Permissions, files, actions, and live updates can then use that shared context. The product gains a consistent answer to who this person is and which part of the application they belong to.
