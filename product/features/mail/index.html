Why it matters

Email becomes more valuable when it stops being an isolated inbox.

A support request, order reply, document, or approval may arrive by email. If the mailbox lives outside the application, people copy information into records and lose the relationship between the message and the work it started.

Daptin can receive SMTP mail for configured accounts and store mailbox and message records. Compatible IMAP clients can read and manage that stored state, while actions and application relationships can build workflows around it.

Attachments can use the same file and cloud-storage foundation as other product content. Outbound work can enter a persisted queue instead of disappearing into an unobservable send call.

Know the boundary

What Daptin leaves in your hands.

Operating internet mail requires DNS, MX routing, reverse DNS, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, reputation, spam and abuse controls, port access, monitoring, and backup. Daptin supplies mail capabilities, not managed deliverability.
