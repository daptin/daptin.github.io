Why it matters

Without history, a surprising record becomes a debate about memory.

A request moves to closed, a customer loses access, or published content changes. The current row shows the outcome but not the path that produced it. Teams end up reconstructing events from messages and logs.

Daptin can generate audit history for selected resources and keeps state transition history for tracked workflows. The evidence remains connected to the underlying record rather than living in a separate spreadsheet.

This gives the team a practical investigation trail while keeping the boundary honest: audit data supports understanding, but retention policy, access, and compliance design remain your responsibility.
