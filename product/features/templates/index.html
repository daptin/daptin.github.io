Why it matters

The same product event often needs more than one human-readable result.

An approved request may need a confirmation page, an email, and a printable record. If every channel builds its own wording and data lookup, the experience drifts and changes become expensive.

Daptin stores reusable templates and can render them with application data inside actions or hosted routes. An action can shape the resulting response, return a file, set headers, or pass the rendered content to mail.

Templates become a bridge between structured backend records and the experiences people actually see, while remaining connected to the workflow that produced them.
