Why it matters

People need answers, not another export-and-spreadsheet ritual.

A support lead wants open requests by team. An operator wants failed jobs by day. A product manager wants published items by category. These are simple questions, but they often create a separate pipeline of exports and bespoke report code.

Daptin provides an aggregation surface over modeled records. It can project, group, filter, order, and combine data so a product or internal dashboard can present useful summaries.

Because the report starts from the same application records, the team does not have to maintain a second definition of what a request, owner, or status means.

Know the boundary

What Daptin leaves in your hands.

The aggregation interface is useful for application and operational reporting. It is not a full analytical warehouse, visualization suite, or substitute for a dedicated BI system when scale and modelling needs demand one.
