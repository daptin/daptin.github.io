Why it matters

Your first internal tool should not delay your first customer-facing feature.

Even a small application needs a way to inspect records, correct data, manage users, and understand what the backend believes. Building that interface can consume weeks before it creates unique product value.

Daptin's administration surface is connected to the live application model. As records and relationships appear, the interface can work with them. Administrators can manage data and reach the actions and configuration that shape the running system.

That makes the admin useful as a starting point, an operational window, and a source of product proof—not a disconnected demo database.
