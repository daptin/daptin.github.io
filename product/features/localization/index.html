Why it matters

A new language should not create a second, disconnected product catalogue.

Teams often duplicate records for each locale, then struggle to keep identifiers, relationships, permissions, and publication state aligned. A small content change becomes a multi-record coordination problem.

Daptin can generate localized data surfaces for resources that enable translations. Language-specific values stay connected to the source record, and browser language can inform which representation a client receives.

That lets product teams add regional experiences while the backend continues to understand one product, article, or category and its shared relationships.

Know the boundary

What Daptin leaves in your hands.

Localization organizes translated application data; it does not translate copy automatically or decide editorial fallback, locale negotiation, cultural adaptation, or translation-review workflow.
