Why it matters

Your storage provider should hold the bytes, not define your product.

A product may begin with local files, move to object storage, or use different providers for different content. If application records depend directly on provider URLs and credentials, every storage decision leaks into the customer experience.

Daptin connects a cloud store to protected credentials and lets asset fields or hosted sites use that store. Records keep the stable application context while the configured backend handles physical content.

This creates a practical separation: product code works with customers, articles, or documents; operators choose where those bytes belong.

01

Match storage to the deployment

Use local paths for fitting installations or rclone-backed providers such as S3-compatible storage where supported.

02

Keep access layered

Separate the customer's record permission from the operator's provider credential.

03

Retain a migration boundary

Move or synchronize content without teaching clients a new object-store contract.

Know the boundary

What Daptin leaves in your hands.

Rclone supports many providers, but that is not a promise that every provider and option is tested. Verify compatibility, credentials, costs, consistency, limits, region, encryption, and recovery for your chosen store.
