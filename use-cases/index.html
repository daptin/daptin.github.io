01 / SAAS

Give every customer a private workspace.

Imagine project software where a customer works with coworkers, uploads documents, and tracks progress. Daptin handles the accounts and makes sure every API result, file, action, and live update stays inside the right workspace. Add plans when accounts need different usage limits.

Customer accountsTeam membersPrivate recordsScheduled remindersChange historyUploads
See how workspace access works →
A good fit when

Your product has organizations, team members, protected data, recurring work, or account-level limits.

02 / OPERATIONS

Replace spreadsheets and inbox handoffs.

Give an operations team one place to manage orders, vendors, approvals, and exceptions. Import the spreadsheet they already have, connect related records, and turn multi-step work such as “approve and notify” into a button the frontend can call.

Admin screensSpreadsheet importsApprovalsReportsExternal accountsChange history
See how records connect →
A good fit when

People are copying data between spreadsheets, email, and several small systems to finish one job.

03 / API BUSINESS

Offer an API with free and paid plans.

Publish data, a business action, or an AI feature to customers. Daptin can identify the caller, apply the plan’s rate and quota limits, record each use, and run your credit or billing action after the call.

Documented APICustomer accountsUsage plansUsage historyHard quotasCredit balances

A good fit when

04 / REALTIME

Update the screen when the work changes.

When one person closes a ticket or changes an order, everyone who may view that record can see the update without reloading. For shared writing, selected document fields can use YJS so several people edit together.

Live dashboardsOrder trackingShared workspacesCollaborative textPublic feedsPermission checks

05 / CONTENT

Keep the catalog, media, and downloads together.

A product can own its photos and manual; an article can own its attachments; a customer can own private documents. Store those files locally or in connected storage, then deliver them through the same access rules as their records.

Product mediaPrivate documentsConnected storageSmall hosted sitesBrowser cachingRSS and Atom

06 / AI & INTEGRATIONS

Let customers connect their tools safely.

A user can connect an external account without sending its token to your frontend. Import the provider’s OpenAPI description and call it through Daptin. For AI features, clients use one OpenAI-compatible address while Daptin selects the provider and records token use.

Connected accountsProtected secretsImported operationsProvider choiceStreaming responsesToken limits
See connected-account flows →
A good fit when

Your frontend needs external data or AI models, but credentials and usage controls must stay on your server.

You do not have to replace the current backend. Use Daptin for the piece that is missing: a protected file library, an operations database, customer OAuth connections, API metering, live record updates, or an OAuth provider for several internal apps.
